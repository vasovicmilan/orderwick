# Progression, trophies and audio

## Card collection
The first card of every family is unlocked from the start. Every other card is earned with *family points*:
+1 for each face-down card of that family you reveal (capped at the number of cards per type on a board,
so undo cannot be farmed), +2 for each type of that family you finish when a board is solved.
Only unlocked cards appear on boards. The menu ticker shows every card in the game; locked ones are `?`.

Tune everything in `config.py` (`POINTS_PER_REVEAL`, `POINTS_PER_SET`, `UNLOCK_THRESHOLDS`).
Progress is saved in `progress.json` (data folder).

## Trophies (achievements)
Art: put a **16x16 PNG** named `assets/achievements/<id>.png` (card tile version) and it is picked up automatically;
until then a placeholder (gold frame + a card) is drawn. Locked trophies show the `?` card.

**Premium cards as rewards:** in `config.py` add `PREMIUM_CARDS = {"gold_grape": "score_100k"}` (sprite `assets/sprites/gold_grape.png`
+ achievement id). When that trophy unlocks, the card is granted and joins boards as its own type.

## Audio
Everything is generated by `tools/make_audio.py` (run it again to regenerate; needs numpy, scipy, ffmpeg), so there is no
third-party licence to track. Files: `assets/audio/sfx/<name>_<n>.wav` (n = pitch variant) and `assets/audio/music/*.ogg`.

Anti-fatigue design:
* every effect has its own loudness (`config.SFX_GAIN`): card move is the reference, pairing is much quieter, a completed column is the loudest;
* pairing has 3 timbres (marimba, kalimba, wooden pluck), random per pair, slight random volume, and the pentatonic scale climbs up and back down;
* every effect has 2-5 pitch variants (about +-8 %); the game never plays the same variant twice in a row;
* pairing cards plays a marimba note of the C-major pentatonic scale that climbs with consecutive pairs (reset after 3 s);
* the music is quiet, drumless marimba/kalimba/pad at 72-84 BPM, eight songs of 95-114 s in shuffled order, with 2-6 s of silence between songs, all normalised to -20 LUFS;
* effects are a little louder than music at the same slider value; volume uses a squared curve.

Settings (Menu > Settings): music on/off + volume, effects on/off + volume; saved in `settings.json`.
Icons for them: `assets/hud/icons_audio.png` (music, sound, mute, gear, trophy, cards; 12x12 each), source in `assets/aseprite/icons_audio.aseprite`.

## Fonts
`Public Pixel` (8 px, UPPERCASE) for titles, buttons, numbers; `Pixel Operator 8` (mixed case, smaller, easier to read) for long text
(help, trophy descriptions, About). Both CC0 - see `assets/fonts/README.txt`.

## About screen
Edit `config.py`: `AUTHOR_NAME`, `VERSION`, `LINKS` (buttons that open in the browser; entries with example.com stay hidden), `CREDITS`.

## Daily challenges
Three tasks a day feed ten extra trophies (`daily_*`, `streak_*`); see `docs/DAILY.md`. They count towards the "all trophies" rule of the Emerald board.
